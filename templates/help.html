Help

Search by a word or phrase first. If no text matches are found, the app will automatically try a reference search as a fallback.

When performing a search, you can choose different search modes such as "All Words", "Any Word", or "Phrase" to refine your search results. Additionally, the Context window setting allows you to specify how many surrounding verses should be displayed along with the search results. These are combined when ranges overlap, when grouped in passage mode, making for better reading and analysis of contextual passage meaning.

Entering a full chapter reference will display all verses within that chapter. The chapter will load in "reading mode", where scrolling up or down load the previous or next chapter automatically. The "New Line Verse" checkbox changes whether the passage displays in paragraph view, or each verse starting on a new line.
