Learn the books

Open Learn the books to practice putting all 66 Bible books in order, from Genesis to Revelation. The books begin shuffled in the cloud. Choose the next book in sequence: correct picks move into Your order, while a wrong pick stays in the cloud, adds a mistake, and tells you which book is next. The timer starts with your first pick. Use Start over to reset the game; after finishing, you can review any books you picked out of order alongside their neighbors.

Your time and optional display name are submitted to the public competition board, which lists the 10 fastest times and 20 most recent finishes. You can play without entering a name. Book colors indicate categories; Acts is grouped with History and Revelation with Prophets.

Search

Enter a word or phrase and choose Search. If the text search finds no matches, the app tries the input as a Bible reference.

  • Search type: Phrase matches the phrase, All Words requires every word, and Any Word matches at least one word.
  • Wildcards: use * to match zero or more letters within a word. For example, love* finds words beginning with “love,” *love finds words ending in “love,” and *love* finds words containing “love.” The wildcard applies only where you put it; it does not stand for spaces or whole phrases.
  • Book group: limit results to a testament or book section. The lists below follow the app's 66-book Protestant Bible order:
    • Old Testament: Genesis–Malachi (books 1–39).
    • New Testament: Matthew–Revelation (books 40–66).
    • Law: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.
    • History: Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 Samuel, 2 Samuel, 1 Kings, 2 Kings, 1 Chronicles, 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther.
    • Poetry: Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon.
    • Prophets: Isaiah, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel, Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.
    • Gospels: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John.
    • Epistles: Romans, 1 Corinthians, 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 Thessalonians, 2 Thessalonians, 1 Timothy, 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, Hebrews, James, 1 Peter, 2 Peter, 1 John, 2 John, 3 John, Jude.
  • Context window: include the selected number of surrounding verses. Overlapping ranges are combined.
  • Case sensitive: match the capitalization in your search.

Choose Reference to look up a reference directly. Enter a chapter (for example, John 3) to open it in reading mode; scroll up or down to load the previous or next chapter.

Choose Multi-reference when you have notes containing more than one Bible reference. Paste the notes into the search box and click the button: the app finds the references and inserts their passages in the same order as the notes. For example, notes such as Read [[John 3:16]]; compare Romans 5:8. can be looked up together. Multi-reference automatically turns on Preserve notes.

Preserve notes keeps the text before, between, and after the references—including text after the final reference—in its original place around the passages. Turn it off when you want the passages without the surrounding notes. Basic Markdown emphasis, inline code, and web links are displayed in preserved notes; Copy retains the original Markdown.

Wikilinks in Markdown

In Display options, set Reference to Wikilink to format a reference like [[John 3.16]]. This is useful when copying results into Markdown notes in apps that support wikilinks, such as Obsidian: the reference becomes a link to a note with that name. Choose Standard for conventional references such as John 3:16, or when your Markdown app does not support wikilinks. The reference-position option controls whether the reference appears before or after the verse text.

What is clickable?

  • Verse references in search results: when Links is on, click a reference to open that verse or chapter in a new tab. Turn Links off to display references as plain text. The verse text itself is not a link.
  • Search insights book bars: click a book to show results from that book. Ctrl-click (Windows/Linux) or Cmd-click (Mac) to add or remove books from the selection; a regular click replaces the current book selection. Click the only selected book again to clear the book filter. Book filters can be combined with word filters and affect the results and word cloud already on the page—they do not start a new search.
  • Search insights word cloud: click a word under “Other words in these verses” to filter the current results to verses containing that word. Select more words to require all selected words; click a selected word again to remove that filter. Ctrl-click (Windows/Linux) or Cmd-click (Mac) a word to open a new search for it in another tab. After a wildcard search, click a word form in “Wildcard word forms” to search for that exact form.
  • Reading view: click a verse number to focus that verse. Use Load previous chapter and Load next chapter to continue reading.
  • Web links in preserved notes: click a displayed link to open its web address.

Display and navigation

  • Group: show results by individual verse or combine them into passages.
  • Reference: choose Wikilink or Standard formatting and put the reference first or last.
  • Red Letter, paragraphs, and Highlight Search: adjust verse styling and search-term highlighting.
  • Links: show or hide links on Bible references.
  • Newline Verse: in passage view, start each verse on a new line.

Display options are available at the bottom of the search page.

Copy and export

Use Copy to copy the results. Choose a format and select Download to export as XLSX, Markdown, DOCX, TXT, or CSV.
